Product Details
This NodeMCU-style ESP32 board features integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, USB Type-C for modern connectivity, and a 30-pin layout including power and ground pins. It is ideal for wireless sensor networks, automation, and embedded IoT applications.

Specifications

Microcontroller: ESP32-WROOM-32 (Xtensa LX6 dual-core @ up to 240 MHz)
Wireless:
- Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n
- Bluetooth v4.2 BR/EDR + BLE
Flash Memory: 4 MB
SRAM: 520 KB
USB Interface: CP2102 USB-to-UART bridge
USB Connector: USB Type-C
GPIO: 30 pins total
Operating Voltage: 3.3 V logic
Power Input: 5 V via USB-C or VIN pin
Current Consumption:
- Active: ~160 mA
- Deep Sleep: ~10 μA
Buttons:
- EN (reset)
- IO0 (boot mode)
Dimensions: ~58 mm × 28 mm
Weight: ~8 g
Programming Support: Arduino IDE, MicroPython, ESP-IDF
OS Compatibility: Windows, Linux, macOS
Features

USB Type-C for reliable flashing and modern power input
Dual-core processor with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
30-pin layout including power and ground for breadboard use
Ideal for IoT, automation, robotics, and wireless sensor networks
Common use cases:
- Smart home controllers and sensors
- BLE/Wi-Fi bridges and gateways
- Data loggers and remote monitoring nodes
- Classroom and STEM embedded projects
